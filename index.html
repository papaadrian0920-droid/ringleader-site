How it works

Set up in one afternoon. Your number stays the same.

  1. Calls you miss come to us

    You turn on call forwarding with your phone company, so calls you don't answer go to your Ringleader line. Customers still dial the number on your truck.

    Within seconds they get one text that names your business and asks what they need. When you're closed, they get your after-hours message instead.

  3. You reply and book

    When they answer, you get a text alert. Reply from your dashboard, mark the job booked, and see how much work Ringleader brought in.

How texting works

We only text people who called first.

These are the rules for the Ringleader missed-call text-back program. They apply to every business that uses Ringleader.

Who gets a text

Only people who call a business that uses Ringleader, when that call isn't answered. Calling the business is how you agree to get the text. We never text bought lists, and we never send ads.

What you'll get

One text right after your missed call. After that, you only get replies in the conversation you started. How many messages depends on that conversation. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP at any time. You'll get one message confirming it, and no more texts after that. Reply START to turn texts back on. Reply HELP for help, or email us.

Your privacy

Your phone number and your agreement to get texts are never sold or shared for marketing. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Read our privacy policy.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number. Only calls you don't answer forward to Ringleader, so customers never notice a change.

Will customers know the first text is automatic?

The first text is automatic and says it's from your business. Every reply after that comes from you.

What about calls late at night?

Callers get your after-hours message, saying you'll text them first thing in the morning. Ringleader never sends follow-up texts on its own.

What if someone doesn't want texts?

They reply STOP, and Ringleader never texts them again unless they reply START.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month, and you can cancel anytime.
